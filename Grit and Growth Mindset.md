# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit


Grit means having passion for a long-term goal and continuing to work towards it even when things get difficult. Talent alone is not enough; consistent effort and perseverance are also important.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset


A growth mindset is believing that we can improve our skills and abilities through learning and effort. Instead of avoiding challenges, mistakes and feedback, we should use them as opportunities to improve.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control


Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and efforts have an influence on the results we get.

The key point in the video is that instead of blaming situations or other people, we should take responsibility for our actions and focus on what we can change. This helps us stay motivated and keep improving.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset


1. Believe in your ability to figure things out.
2. Question your assumptions.
3. Create your own curriculum for growth.
4. Accept and learn from struggles.

## 5. My Ideas to Take Action and Build a Growth Mindset


1. Take responsibility for my own learning instead of depending on others.
2. Practice coding regularly and keep working on problems even when they are difficult.
3. Focus on understanding concepts instead of just completing tasks.
4. Treat mistakes and errors as learning opportunities.
5. Take feedback positively and work on improving myself.
6. Use documentation and other resources to find solutions before asking for help.
7. Take ownership of the projects I work on and complete them properly.
8. Set small goals, track my progress and stay consistent.
9. Focus on improving my skills instead of comparing myself with others.
10. Stay patient and remember that improvement takes time.
