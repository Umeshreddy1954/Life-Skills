# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique?

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you explain a concept in simple words, as if teaching someone who has no knowledge of that topic, to identify gaps in your understanding.

## Question 2: Most interesting story or idea

The story that interested me the most was Barbara Oakley's explanation of Thomas Edison and how he used to come up with new ideas. Whenever he got stuck on a problem, he would sit back and relax while holding ball bearings in his hand. As he started falling asleep, the ball bearings would drop and wake him up. This helped him capture the ideas that came to his mind during that relaxed state.

What I found interesting about this is that taking a break doesn't necessarily mean we are wasting time. Sometimes, when we stop thinking too much about a problem and do something else, our brain continues to process it in the background. When we return to it, we might come up with a completely different approach or solution.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Focused mode:** This is when we concentrate completely on a particular task. For example, when I'm learning a new Java concept, reading documentation, or debugging code, I'm using focused thinking. It helps us understand things logically and work through problems step by step.

- **Diffuse mode:** This is when we are relaxed and not actively concentrating on anything specific. Activities like walking, taking a shower, or simply taking a break can help us enter this mode. It allows our brain to make connections and sometimes come up with ideas that we couldn't think of while concentrating.

- **Why both matter:** Both modes are important for learning. We need focused mode to understand and work on a problem, but we also need breaks to let our minds process things. Switching between the two can help us learn better and approach problems from different perspectives.

## Question 5: What are some actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

Going forward, I want to improve the way I learn by being more involved in the process rather than just watching videos or reading concepts. I want to focus on understanding things properly, applying them in practice, and being consistent.

- **Use the Feynman Technique:** Whenever I learn a new concept, I'll try to explain it in my own words, either by writing it down or teaching someone else. If I struggle to explain something, I'll go back and work on the areas I haven't understood properly.

- **Understand before implementing:** Before writing code, I'll try to understand where a concept is used, why we need it, and how it works. Then I'll implement it through small coding examples and projects.

- **Practice consistently:** I'll make sure that I spend time implementing what I learn instead of limiting myself to theory. I'll also complete the practice drills and assignments related to each topic.

- **Improve my concentration:** I'll keep my phone on silent, turn off unnecessary notifications, and avoid social media during study hours. I'll try to give my complete attention to whatever I'm working on.

- **Follow deep work:** I'll work in dedicated sessions without switching between multiple tasks. I'll follow the principle of working when it's time to work and taking breaks when it's time to relax.

- **Take intentional breaks:** Instead of continuously studying for long hours, I'll take short breaks in between. This will help me refresh my mind and sometimes approach problems from a different perspective.

- **Break bigger topics into smaller parts:** Whenever I start learning something new, I'll divide it into smaller sections and focus on understanding one part at a time rather than trying to learn everything together.

- **Set realistic deadlines:** I'll assign deadlines for completing topics, practice drills, and projects. I'll use these deadlines to manage my time and maintain consistency rather than rushing through concepts.

- **Track my time and progress:** I'll track how much time I spend learning and practising. This will help me understand how effectively I'm using my time and where I need to improve.

- **Communicate proactively:** If I face difficulties or get stuck on something, I'll communicate the problem and ask for help instead of waiting until it affects my progress.

- **Focus on depth over breadth:** Instead of trying to learn too many technologies at once, I'll focus on mastering a few important skills. I believe having a strong understanding of the fundamentals is more valuable than knowing a little about everything.

- **Make learning enjoyable:** I'll approach new concepts with curiosity, explore where they are used in real-world applications, and try to understand them in my own way. This will make learning more interesting and help me remember things better.

My main goal is to become someone who can understand concepts clearly, explain them confidently, implement them properly, and complete assigned work on time.
