# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment can include any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature, whether verbal, visual or physical. Some examples are:

- Making inappropriate comments about someone's appearance, clothing or body.
- Telling sexual or offensive jokes, making suggestive remarks or using vulgar language.
- Repeatedly asking someone out even after they have said no.
- Unwanted physical contact such as hugging, touching, kissing or standing too close.
- Sharing or displaying sexually inappropriate pictures, videos, messages or emails.
- Staring, making unwanted gestures or passing sexual comments.
- Offering promotions or other work-related benefits in exchange for sexual favours, or threatening someone's job for refusing them.

It can also include creating an uncomfortable or hostile work environment through repeated inappropriate behaviour. Sexual harassment can happen between people of any gender. Even if something seems like a joke to one person, it may make someone else uncomfortable.

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face such behaviour, I would clearly communicate that it is inappropriate and ask the person to stop, provided I feel comfortable doing so. I would also keep a record of the incidents, including dates, times, locations and any witnesses.

If the behaviour continues, or if I am not comfortable confronting the person, I would report it to the company's Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) or the appropriate authority.

If I witness such an incident, I would support the affected person, avoid ignoring or encouraging the behaviour, and report it through the proper channel if necessary.

I believe it is everyone's responsibility to maintain a respectful and safe workplace where people feel comfortable speaking up without fear of retaliation.
